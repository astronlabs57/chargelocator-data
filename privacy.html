Privacy Policy — ChargeLocator

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Astron Labs ("we", "us", "our") operates the ChargeLocator mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS to find nearby electric vehicle charging stations. Your location is processed on your device only and is sent to third-party APIs (OpenChargeMap, OpenStreetMap/Overpass) solely to retrieve charging station data near you. We do not store, log, or transmit your location to our own servers.

1.2 Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Favorite charging stations you have saved
  • Vehicle profile settings (connector type preferences, minimum power)
  • Cached charging station data for offline use

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us.

1.3 Advertising Data

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and process the following data for ad personalization and delivery:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Approximate location (IP-based)
  • App usage data and ad interaction data

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

1.4 Consent Data

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage your advertising consent preferences in accordance with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive. Your consent choice is stored locally on your device.

1.5 Crash Reports

If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives a crash report so we can fix the error: the stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk space, and a random installation ID generated by Crashlytics. Crash reports contain no location, no search queries and no advertising ID. They are kept for 90 days.

1.6 Rating Prompt

After a few routes the App may show Google Play's rating dialog. It is provided by Google Play; we receive no data from it (ratings appear in the Play Store under your Google account, as with any app).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not require user accounts or registration
  • We do not operate our own servers or databases
  • We do not track your usage behavior or create user profiles
  • We do not share any data with third parties beyond what is described in Section 1.3

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — You can request information about the data processed by the App.
  • Right to deletion — You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.
  • Right to withdraw consent — You can change your advertising consent at any time via the "Privacy Settings" option in the App's Vehicle Profile menu.
  • Right to object — You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, most data subject requests are fulfilled by clearing the App's local data.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. The App's advertising is configured with setTagForUnderAgeOfConsent(false).

6. Data Retention

All data stored by the App resides on your device. Cached charging station data is automatically refreshed and expired data is deleted. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
